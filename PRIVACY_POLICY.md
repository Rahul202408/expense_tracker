# Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker: Money Manager

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026

Welcome to **Expense Tracker: Money Manager** ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal financial data remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
When you register an account, we may collect:
- **Full Name**
- **Email Address**
- **Phone Number** (for verification and account recovery)

### B. Financial & Transaction Data
All income and expense records, categories, budgets, and financial history you input are stored securely in **Google Firebase Cloud Firestore** to sync across your devices.

### C. Device & Security Data
- Biometric authentication status (processed locally on your device for PIN/Fingerprint lock).
- Device identifiers used by Google AdMob to deliver ads.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly for:
- Providing and maintaining the core expense tracking and budget analytics features.
- Authenticating your identity securely via Firebase Auth.
- Sending important budget threshold warnings (e.g., 80% or 100% budget alerts) via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
- Serving non-intrusive advertisements via **Google AdMob**.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- **Data Encryption:** All data transmitted between your app and server is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS.
- **Firebase Security:** Your financial data is protected by strict Firebase Security Rules, ensuring only you can access your records.
- **No Third-Party Sale:** We **NEVER** sell, rent, or trade your personal or financial data to third-party advertisers or marketers.

---

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our app displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect device advertising IDs and anonymized usage data to serve relevant ads according to Google's Privacy Policy:
- [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

---

## 5. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You have full control over your data:
- **Account Deletion:** You can delete your account and all associated transaction records at any time through the Profile settings in the app or by contacting support.
- **Ad Preferences:** You can reset your device's Advertising Identifier in your Android device settings.
