Privacy Policy for Expense Tracker: Money Manager

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Welcome to Expense Tracker: Money Manager ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal financial data remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number (for authentication and security)

B. Financial & Transaction Data

All income and expense records, categories, budgets, and financial history you input are stored securely in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore to sync across your devices.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Providing core expense tracking, analytics, and budget management.
  • Authenticating your identity securely via Firebase Auth.
  • Sending budget warning notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Serving advertisements via Google AdMob.

3. Data Storage & Security

We use SSL/TLS encryption for all data in transit. Your financial data is protected by Firebase Security Rules. We NEVER sell or share your personal data with third-party marketers.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our app uses Google AdMob for ads. AdMob may collect anonymous device advertising identifiers in accordance with Google Advertising Privacy Policy.

5. Your Choices & Data Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated transaction records at any time through the Profile settings in the app.
