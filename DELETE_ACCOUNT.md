## How to Request Account & Data Deletion

At **Expense Tracker: Money Manager**, we respect your right to control your personal and financial data. You can request the deletion of your account and all associated data at any time using either of the following methods:

---

### Method 1: Delete Account Inside the Mobile App (Instant)
1. Open the **Expense Tracker: Money Manager** app on your phone.
2. Go to the **Profile** tab from the bottom navigation bar.
3. Scroll down and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Confirm your deletion request.
5. Your account, authentication credentials, and all stored transactions will be **permanently deleted immediately**.

---

### Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Within 48 Hours)
If you are unable to access the app, you can submit a deletion request via email:
1. Send an email to **rplaystore2026@gmail.com**.
2. Use the subject line: **"Account Deletion Request - Expense Tracker"**.
3. Include your registered **Email Address** and **Phone Number** in the email body.
4. Our support team will process your request and permanently delete all your data within **48 hours**, and send you a confirmation email.

---

## What Data is Deleted?

Upon account deletion, the following data is **permanently wiped** from our Google Firebase servers:
- **Personal Information:** Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
- **Financial Data:** All Income entries, Expense entries, Custom Categories, and Monthly Budgets.
- **Authentication Records:** Firebase Auth User Account & Credentials.

---

## Data Retention Policy
We do not retain any user financial or personal data after account deletion. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
